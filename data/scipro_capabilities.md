## Our offering (hiring models)
- Direct hire: permanent hires for long-term success
- Executive search: leadership hires, including retained search ("a tailored solution and
  exclusive engagement")
- Contract recruitment: flexible staffing for short-term and project-based needs
- Embedded solution: dedicated talent partners working inside the client's team
- Multi hire: building several roles or a whole team at once
- Project delivery: project-based talent solutions
- Contingent search: no upfront fees

## Therapeutic-area clients (named in SciPro's public case studies)

## How SciPro works (recurring themes in the public case studies)
- Market mapping and proactive (passive) talent engagement
- Weekly progress updates and pipeline reviews with HR and hiring managers
- Acts as an extension of the internal talent team
- Fills niche roles that have been open for months
