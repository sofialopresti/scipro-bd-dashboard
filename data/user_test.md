# User test: SciPro BD Dashboard

Tester: Sofia Lopresti, former SciPro 360 recruiter (business development + delivery)
Date: 2026-10-23

The manual times are **estimates from my experience doing this job at SciPro, not timed**.
The app times are measured AI generation times from the traced run on 2026-10-23
(they don't include the recruiter's review and editing time).

| # | Task | By hand (estimate) | App (measured AI time) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find 5 biotech companies worth prospecting | 60-90 min | 30-60 s |
| 2 | Research + write one personalized outreach email | 30-45 min | ~11 s (draft + 1 revision) |
| - | Find the right person and their email (not in the tool) | 15 min | not covered |
| 3 | Build a tailored pitch | 60-90 min | ~13 s |
| 4a | Write the job spec | 30 min | ~15 s for 4a-4c |
| 4b | Build the interview scorecard | 15-20 min | |
| 4c | Build the sourcing plan (target companies + boolean searches) | 1-2 hours | |
|   | **One company end to end (excluding contact finding)** | **about 4-6.5 hours** | **about 1.5 min + review** |

**Quote:** "I used to do this for real... this process would take me 5+ hours start to finish, probably."

**Standout feature:** the sourcing plan. Knowing where the right people work and writing the boolean
searches is one of the hardest parts of a search.
